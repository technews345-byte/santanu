Product

Fresh bowls, made easy to order.

Everything you need to go from hungry to a fresh bowl at your door in Sonari or Nazira — built to be quick on your phone.

A menu that is always current

Six fresh bowls in regular and large sizes, from ₹99. Prices, sizes and sold-out bowls update the moment our kitchen changes them — what you see is what we can make today.

Order in under a minute

Add bowls to your order, pick a size and check out on the website. No app to install and no account or password to remember.

Delivery priced by distance

Share your location and we work out the distance to the nearest kitchen and the exact delivery charge before you pay. Self pickup is always free.

Choose your delivery window

Pick a morning or evening slot for today or the next few days. We only offer times the kitchen can actually deliver.

Pay your way

Pay online with UPI, cards or net banking through Razorpay, or pay cash when your bowls arrive or when you pick them up.

Live order tracking

Every order gets a private tracking link. Follow it from confirmed to preparing, out for delivery and delivered — no login needed.

Notifications at every step

Turn on updates on your tracking page and your phone is notified when your order is confirmed, prepared, on the way and delivered. Questions? Our help desk is on WhatsApp.

Offers and coupons

Current offers are applied automatically at checkout and coupon codes are checked instantly, so you always see the final price before you order.

Honest reviews

After your order is delivered you can rate it from your tracking page. Only real customers who ordered can leave a review.

Made for the kitchen too

The same system runs our kitchen.

Your order goes straight to our team with an alert, so it is confirmed and prepared without a phone call in between.

  • Prices checked by our serverTotals, discounts and delivery charges are calculated by Bowl Mania, never taken from the browser.
  • Payments verifiedOnline payments are confirmed with Razorpay before your order is marked as paid.
  • Delivery partners in the loopOur delivery team sees only what they need: the address, the order and the time.
