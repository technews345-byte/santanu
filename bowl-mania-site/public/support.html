Ordering

How do I place an order?

Open the menu, choose a bowl and size, and tap Add. Open your order, choose delivery or pickup, pick a time, add your details and place the order. You don't need an app or an account.

You can also send your order to us on WhatsApp.

Is there a minimum order?

If a minimum order applies, it is shown in your order before you check out. Pickup orders never have a delivery charge.

Can I customise my bowl or tell you about an allergy?

Yes. Use Notes for the kitchen at checkout (for example "less spicy" or "paneer instead of chicken") and we will do our best. Our bowls can contain nuts, seeds, dairy, egg, gluten and soy, and they are all prepared in the same kitchen. If you have a serious allergy, please WhatsApp us before ordering.

Do you have vegetarian bowls?

Yes. Every bowl on the menu is marked veg or non-veg. The Grill Power Bowl can be made with chicken or paneer.

Do you take bulk or party orders?

Yes — please send a request through the contact form or our help desk at least a day ahead with the number of bowls and the time you need them.

Delivery & pickup

Where do you deliver?

We deliver around our kitchens in Sonari and Nazira. At checkout, share your location and we tell you straight away whether we deliver there and from which kitchen.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is charged by distance from the kitchen: ₹10 for the first 1.5 km, then ₹10 for every additional 1.5 km. The exact charge for your address is shown before you pay. Pickup is free.

When do you deliver?

Sonari: 7 – 9 AM and 4 – 8 PM. Nazira: 7 – 11 AM and 4 – 8 PM. You choose a delivery window at checkout; you can also order ahead for the next few days.

Why do you ask for my location?

Only to measure the distance to the kitchen, calculate the delivery charge and help our delivery partner find you. See our Privacy Policy.

Payments & refunds

How can I pay?

Online with UPI, debit or credit cards or net banking through Razorpay, or in cash on delivery or at pickup. The options available are shown at checkout.

My payment failed but money was deducted. What now?

Don't worry — your order is only confirmed once Razorpay confirms the payment to us. If money left your account for a failed payment, it is returned to you automatically by your bank or Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days.

If you are unsure, send us your order number and we will check the payment for you.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Call or WhatsApp the kitchen as soon as possible. We can change or cancel an order before we start preparing it. Once your bowls are being prepared we usually cannot cancel, because everything is made fresh for you. See our Terms of Service.

How do refunds work?

If we cancel your order, or something is wrong with it, we refund online payments to the original payment method. Refunds normally reach you within 5–7 working days after we issue them.

How do I use a coupon?

Enter the code in Coupon code at checkout and tap Apply. Automatic offers are applied for you. The discount is shown before you pay.

Tracking & problems

How do I track my order?

After ordering you get a private tracking link (tap Turn on on that page to get a notification on your phone at every step). Lost it? Use Track order with your order number and the mobile number you ordered with.

My order is late or something is missing.

We're sorry! Please WhatsApp or call the kitchen you ordered from with your order number and we will sort it out right away.

How do I leave a review?

Once your order is delivered, open your tracking link and rate your bowls. Reviews are checked before they appear on the website.

Your account & data

Do I need an account?

No. We recognise your orders by the mobile number you order with — there is no password to create or remember.

How do I delete my account and data?

Send a request from our Delete Account page. We confirm it is you and then erase your personal details within 30 days.

What do you do with my details?

We use them to prepare and deliver your order, send you order updates and help you if something goes wrong. We never sell your data. Read the Privacy Policy.
