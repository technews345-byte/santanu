Who we are

Bowl Mania ("we", "us") runs healthy food kitchens in Sonari and Nazira, Assam, India, and this website for ordering our bowls. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the website, order from us or contact us, why we use it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Questions about this policy: WhatsApp or call Sonari on +91 80990 26415 or Nazira on +91 87209 15865, or use our contact form.

How we use your data

  • To take, prepare, deliver and support your orders.
  • To send order confirmations and status updates, including on WhatsApp.
  • To answer your questions, feedback and requests.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse and keep the website secure.
  • To keep the business records that tax and accounting laws require.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which bowls and areas are popular so we can improve.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Why we are allowed to use it

We process your data with your consent, which you give when you place an order or send us a message, and for legitimate uses allowed by law — such as fulfilling the order you asked for and meeting legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Your rights); this does not affect orders already completed.

Who we share it with

Only with people and services that help us serve you, and only what they need:

  • Our staff and delivery partners — your name, phone, address and order, to prepare and deliver it.
  • Razorpay (payment gateway) — to process online payments and refunds. See Razorpay's privacy policy.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business) — to send you order messages and receive your replies.
  • Hosting and email providers — that run our servers and send emails on our behalf.
  • Authorities — when the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

The website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps the bowls in your order so they are still there if you reload the page; you can clear it any time in your browser. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. Our staff admin area uses essential sign-in cookies that are only set for staff.

How long we keep it

  • Orders and payment records — for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Your customer profile, saved addresses and location — while you keep ordering from us, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Messages — until your query is resolved and for a reasonable time after, in case you follow up.
  • Security logs — for a short period needed to protect the service.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it — use our Delete Account page;
  • stop sending you WhatsApp updates.

You can also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. We will confirm your identity using the mobile number you ordered with and respond within 30 days.

How we protect it

The website uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Payments are handled by Razorpay. Only authorised staff can see customer details, each with their own sign-in and permissions, and changes are logged. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affecting you ever happens we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Our website is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to order for you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services change. The date at the top shows when it last changed; important changes will also be shown on the website.

Contact and grievances

For any privacy question, request or complaint, WhatsApp or call Sonari on +91 80990 26415 or Nazira on +91 87209 15865, or use our contact form. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
