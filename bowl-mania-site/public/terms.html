About these terms

These terms apply when you use the Bowl Mania website or order from Bowl Mania in Sonari or Nazira, Assam. By placing an order you agree to them. If you have a question, WhatsApp or call Sonari on +91 80990 26415 or Nazira on +91 87209 15865, or use our contact form.

Placing an order

  • An order is placed when you see the confirmation with your order number. For online payment, the order is confirmed only after Razorpay confirms your payment.
  • Please give an accurate name, mobile number and delivery location. We may call you to confirm details.
  • We may decline or cancel an order — for example if a bowl has sold out, the address is outside our delivery area, or we cannot reach you. If you already paid, you get a full refund.

Prices and payment

  • Prices are in Indian Rupees and are shown on the menu and at checkout. The total at checkout — including any discount, delivery charge and tax — is the amount you pay.
  • You can pay online through Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking) or in cash on delivery or at pickup, when those options are offered.
  • If a price is shown incorrectly because of an obvious error, we will contact you before preparing the order.

Delivery and pickup

  • We deliver within set distances of our Sonari and Nazira kitchens. The delivery charge depends on distance and is shown before you pay.
  • You choose a delivery window at checkout. We aim to deliver within it, but times can be affected by weather, traffic or demand; we will keep you updated.
  • Please be available at the address and phone number you gave. If we cannot deliver after reasonable attempts, the order may be treated as delivered.
  • Pickup orders should be collected during the chosen window.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

  • You can change or cancel an order by calling or WhatsApping the kitchen before we start preparing it. Because every bowl is made fresh, orders already being prepared generally cannot be cancelled.
  • If we cancel an order, or an item is missing, wrong or not of acceptable quality, tell us on the same day with your order number (a photo helps). We will replace the item or refund it.
  • Refunds for online payments go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 5–7 working days after we issue them. Cash payments are refunded in cash or by UPI.

Food, allergies and dietary needs

  • Our bowls are freshly prepared and best eaten soon after delivery. Keep any leftovers refrigerated.
  • Our kitchen handles nuts, seeds, dairy, egg, gluten, soy and chicken. We cannot guarantee any bowl is free from allergens. If you have an allergy, tell us before ordering.
  • Veg and non-veg bowls are marked on the menu. Photos are for illustration; toppings can vary slightly with season.

Offers and coupons

Offers and coupons have their own conditions (such as minimum order, dates or usage limits), which are checked at checkout. They cannot be exchanged for cash and we may end an offer at any time. We may cancel an order that misuses a coupon.

Using the website

  • Use the website only to browse and order for yourself or others with their permission.
  • Do not try to interfere with the website, access others' orders, or submit false orders.
  • Tracking links are private to your order — share them only with people you trust.
  • Reviews must be honest and about your own order. By posting a review you allow us to show it on our website and social media with your first name. We may remove reviews that are offensive or unrelated.

Privacy

Law and disputes

Changes to these terms
