Delete your account and data.

You don't need a password to order from us — your "account" is the details we keep from your orders. You can ask us to erase them at any time.

What happens when you ask

  1. Send the request
    Use the form with the mobile number you order with.
  2. We confirm it's you
    We contact that mobile number, so nobody else can delete your data.
  3. We erase your data
    Within 30 days, and we let you know when it is done.

We delete

  • Your customer profileName, mobile number, email and notes about you.
  • Addresses and locationsSaved addresses and the location of every past delivery.
  • Messages and reviewsContact messages and reviews you have written.

What we keep: order numbers, items and amounts, with your name and contact details removed, because tax and accounting laws require us to keep sales records. We also keep a record that you asked for deletion.

Orders that are still in progress must be delivered or cancelled first. After deletion, your old tracking links stop showing your details and past orders can no longer be looked up with your number.
