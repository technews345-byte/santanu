Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

The short version. Spendly keeps your money data on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to sign in, and even then your receipt photos stay on the device. Your financial records are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Who this is about

Spendly is a personal expense and budget app for Android, built by Santanu Bordoloi. This policy covers the Spendly app and nothing else. Questions go to the email at the bottom of this page.

What stays on your device

Everything you record is written to a database on the phone itself:

  • Transactions — amounts, dates, notes, categories and accounts
  • Accounts and balances
  • Categories and budgets
  • Receipt photos you attach
  • App settings, such as your theme and whether the biometric lock is on

If you use Spendly without signing in, this is the whole story — nothing leaves the phone, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional and exists so your data survives losing your phone. Spendly uses Google Firebase for this.

What Where it goes
Google sign-in — your name, email address, profile photo and Google account identifier Firebase Authentication
Phone sign-in — your phone number Firebase Authentication
Your transactions, accounts, categories and budgets Cloud Firestore, stored under your own account identifier
Receipt photos Nowhere. They are never uploaded — only a reference to the file on your phone is synced

Security rules on the database restrict every read and write to the signed-in account that owns the records. No other Spendly user can reach your data, and it is not pooled or analysed.

If you sign in by phone number, note that Google states it sends and stores phone numbers given for authentication in order to improve spam and abuse prevention across Google services.

Advertising

Spendly shows ads through Google AdMob: one when the app is opened, and one you can choose to watch from the Support section in Settings. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information and whether you interacted with an ad.

Your financial data is never used for advertising and is never sent to AdMob. Google's handling of ad data is covered by Google's own privacy terms. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and turn off ad personalisation, under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android phone.

What Spendly does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking — the app collects no statistics about how you use it
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anybody
  • No connection to your bank; Spendly cannot see your real accounts and you enter everything yourself
  • No biometric data — fingerprint and face unlock are handled entirely by Android, and Spendly only ever learns whether the phone accepted you

Exports

PDF and Excel reports are generated on your phone. Where they go afterwards is your choice — Spendly hands the file to Android's share sheet and nothing else.

Deleting your data

  • On the phone — uninstalling Spendly removes the local database and everything in it
  • In the cloud — write to the address below and your account and its synced records will be deleted

Children

Spendly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes
