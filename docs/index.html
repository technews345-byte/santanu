The look

Rounded glass

An iOS-inspired interface of frosted glass panels over a deep blue background, in dark and light.

Floating tab bar

Home, Activity, Budgets, Analytics and Settings in a floating glass capsule, with a soft spring on every tap.

Clear category symbols

One family of outline icons across budgets, entries, activity and charts.

Calm motion

Native-driven springs that stay smooth on the phone, and switch off when Reduce Motion is on.

What it does

Track spending

Income, expenses, transfers and investments, with categories, accounts and receipt photos.

Scan receipts

Photograph or pick a receipt; the amount, date and shop are read on the phone itself.

Set budgets

Give a category a monthly limit and watch how much of it is left as the month goes on.

See the shape of it

Charts for where the money went, how it trends, and what your net position looks like.

Take it with you

Export a formatted PDF report or an Excel sheet whenever you need one.

Private and locked

Everything lives on your phone, with fingerprint or face unlock. Sign in with Google only if you want a cloud backup.

Privacy

Your financial records stay on the device unless you sign in, receipt photos are never uploaded at all, and nothing you record is used for advertising or sold to anyone. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
